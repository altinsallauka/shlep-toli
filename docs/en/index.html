FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Towing in Kosovo. What you need to know.

TOLI in Bellanicë, Malishevë, Kosovo: towing, flatbed vehicle transport and crane recovery.

How can I request towing in Kosovo?+

Call TOLI on +383 44 116 446 or contact us on WhatsApp. Share your exact location, vehicle condition and destination. We will confirm transport availability and the details with you.

Where is Shlep Auto Bartje TOLI based?+

We are based in Bellanicë, Malishevë 24000, Kosovo. Open the Google Maps link in our contact section for directions. Contact us to arrange transport from another location.

Is roadside assistance available 24 hours?+

You can call us day or night for roadside assistance. Arrival time depends on your location and availability; we confirm it during the call.

How much does vehicle towing cost?+

The price depends on the distance, vehicle type and condition, and the equipment required. Send us the details for a quote and agree on the price before transport.

Can you transport sports cars and damaged vehicles?+

Our photos show sports cars transported on flatbeds and damaged vehicles recovered with a crane. Tell us your model and its condition so we can confirm the appropriate equipment.
